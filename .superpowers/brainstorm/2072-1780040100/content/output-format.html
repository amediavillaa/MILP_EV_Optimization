What output formats do you need?

For an academic paper the format affects figure quality and table inclusion. Pick all that apply.

A

PDF / vector figures

Lossless at any zoom. Required by most IEEE/Elsevier submissions. Matplotlib can export .pdf directly.

B

High-res PNG (300 dpi)

Raster at print resolution. Simpler to embed in Word or Overleaf without PDF figure quirks.

C

LaTeX tables (.tex)

Booktabs-style \begin{tabular} blocks ready to \input{} into your paper.

D

CSV tables

Raw data for the tables, useful for double-checking values or converting to another format later.
