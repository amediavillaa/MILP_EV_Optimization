What column layout is your paper?

This sets the figure width so PDFs render crisply without scaling artefacts in the typeset paper.

A

IEEE two-column (recommended)

Single-column figures: 3.5 in wide. Double-span figures: 7.16 in wide. Standard for IEEE Transactions / Access / conferences.

B

Elsevier / single-column

Full-width figures: 6.5–7 in. Used in Elsevier journals (Applied Energy, Energy, etc.) and single-column drafts.

C

Springer / LNCS

Figures typically 4.5–5 in wide. Used in Springer conference proceedings and journals.
